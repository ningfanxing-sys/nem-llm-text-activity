## 1. Interpretation, population and access boundary

This is a historical confirmation using windows that the author states have never informed design decisions in this study. It is not prospective. Portions of 2026 were encountered elsewhere in the wider project; this is disclosed rather than called universally untouched. Window C remains an already exposed historical transfer evaluation and is not reused as confirmation. The two external MS10 reviews and round-5 synthesis motivate direct activity-controlled contrasts and an independent final evaluation; their earlier results select nothing within CONF.

All times below are **fixed AEST (UTC+10)**, including NSW daylight-saving dates; no changing local Sydney offset. News issues are 00:00 on each date 2026-03-01 through 2026-09-30 inclusive: 214 issue days, NSW1/QLD1/TAS1, the same three-region population as NEWSD. At midnight on day d, predict the 24 hourly prices of day d (operationally next delivery day from the prior day's information). These are not day d+1 targets. Its nominal grid is 214×3×24 = 15,408 keys. The last hourly target requires dispatch interval-end keys through 2026-10-01 00:00 AEST; DATA26 reports those boundary sources.

Notices retain RF2's **target-day** convention: target timestamps in [2026-08-01 00:00, 2026-10-01 00:00) AEST, all five NEM regions, 5/10/15/20/25/30-minute horizons, issue=target−horizon. This gives exactly 61 target-day inferential units; late July 31 issues for early August targets belong to the fixed grid. Source/panel build is July 27–September 30 with July 27–31 lead-in. The only notice evaluation-window sensitivity drops August 1–7 and rescores the same predictions on August 8–September 30 (54 target days). No additional period or refit sensitivity is permitted. Target/interval conventions and eligible keys must be frozen and recorded before values, with no outcome-selected rows.

The prohibited design reads are confirmation prices/RRP, demand/physical values, operator forecasts/P5MIN values, prediction values and target/label outcomes. Only keys, clocks, schema, row counts and hashes may be inspected from their containers. Opaque streaming SHA-256 is not decoding. OPENED_PATHS.jsonl logs opened project/input/document paths; worker network source accesses are also recorded. Raw news text is an extraction input, not a numeric outcome panel; one body-containing JSON was mechanically decoded during metadata preparation, without examination of confirmation bodies. The precise access limitation is documented in BLINDING_ACCESS_NOTE.md. We do not claim that no confirmation text bytes were decoded. No new notice extraction is executed here; NRX is an independent in-progress producer, read only for contract/identity.

## 3. News training and report-only validation

Fit Bai vocabulary, lexical filtering, ADF/differencing/Granger/lag selection and any encoder-independent fitted preprocessing **only on 2021-10-01–2024-12-31 training**, with labels available before 2025-01-01 00:00 AEST. Use source histories before October only as lookback, not as target training days. The MiniLM encoder is pretrained and frozen, never fitted to project prices; its tokenizer and normalisation are frozen. LightGBM uses native NaN handling; freeze deterministic constant/all-missing pruning from this nominal training support rather than reselecting preprocessing on confirmation. No vocabulary or feature pruning is learned from 2025/2026.

2025 is already seen and is **report-only validation**. One end-2024 training fit per base LightGBM configuration produces the year-long validation report using issue-legal inputs, analogous to NEWSD's single validation fit with changed dates. It selects no hyperparameter, representation, threshold, seed, stopping rule or arm. No additional daily 2025 refitting programme is required. Historical NEWSD reports remain context, not validation of the new representations. A missing validation artifact never authorizes using confirmation to choose parameters.

At each 2026 confirmation midnight, fit one pooled model per news configuration on the expanding set of past issue-day examples from October 2021 onward whose **complete 3-region×24-hour target-day labels have recorded availability strictly before that origin**. This includes legal 2025/January–February 2026 history and earlier confirmation days as they become legal, after design/input freeze; no future label or equality-boundary label enters. Existing DAPANEL ends in December 2025, so January–February bridges must be bound before execution rather than silently omitted. Build features at each historical example's own issue clock. Preprocessing remains fitted only through 2024; daily learner refits are not preprocessing reselection.

Keep NEWSD's direct hourly-price MSE LightGBM: 180 trees, 15 leaves, depth 5, learning rate .04, minimum child samples 100, column subsample .85, subsample .9 with frequency 0, lambda 1, deterministic/force-col-wise true, one thread. Primary seed is 20260919. There is no target transform, early stopping, capacity search or outcome tuning. N is the same 72-hour numeric history/masks and known calendar/region/horizon input as NEWSD; no P5MIN or target-day exogenous forecast enters N. See DATA_BUILD_PLAN.md for exact slot clocks and fields.

Arms are N; M=N+matched 72-hour article activity and shared extraction-failure status; L_count=M+the fixed 34-column NEWSX v4 projection; L_chen=M+Chen-ordered extraction serialisation MiniLM-384; E_raw=M+same-support raw title/body MiniLM-384; W_Bai=M+official Bai word-frequency projection. Every content arm contains M. Raw and structured arms use exactly the same source IDs/versions. Terminal failed extraction is UNKNOWN with the same engineering failure mask in M and all content arms, never an L-only row deletion. Exact serialisation, normalisation, missingness and Bai empty-vocabulary handling are in FEATURE_SPEC_CONF.md. The Bai official thresholds are not lowered if empty: report W_Bai unavailable. No top-500/lag-30 replacement is registered.

Main news window is (issue−72h,issue] using the frozen saved-version availability proxy. Only the 24-hour window is a window sensitivity, and it simultaneously changes M/L_count/L_chen support. It does not compare 24-hour M with 72-hour content. Only L_chen and E_raw have the first-L-token overflow sensitivity; the main rule covers the entire concatenated input by nonoverlapping content-token chunks, averaging their vectors and L2 normalising. Neither sensitivity replaces a main arm. The encoder's pinned effective length is checked rather than guessed; see ENCODER_LOCK.json.

Where the full source prefix has a missing intervening history (notably March–July), retain its status as **observed saved-source prefix**, not certified all-issued prefix. This limits M_notice and Chen-history interpretation, and unresolved events from an unobserved gap cannot be claimed as known active states. Cross-gap unclosed events with no fresh legal observation are UNKNOWN; bounded future schedules explicitly supported by a pre-gap source may remain known under the adopted assembler.

The author fixes July 27 onward as the source-supported interval; this decision is not reopened. Require complete keys/clocks/raw-source identities and NRX success/failure coverage **relative to the frozen DATA26 canonical registry** for July 27–September 30, including the lead-in, before features. ## 5. Registered benchmark and secondary controls

**LEAR is a numerical benchmark, not a text arm.** Follow Lago et al., Applied Energy 293 (2021), 116983, PDF pp.7–9,14: hourly LASSO, historical LARS/AIC regularisation selection then coordinate-descent refit, median/MAD asinh variance stabilisation fitted on each legal calibration sample, daily recalibration, 56/84/1092/1456-day windows and equal averaging of the four window forecasts. Details and implementation-version differences are frozen in LEAR_SPEC.md. Use the same three news regions and issue-legal labels. No day-d exogenous forecasts exist: omit the corresponding exogenous forecast blocks explicitly; do not insert realized weather, demand or operator forecasts. Legal price lags 1/2/3/7 days and weekdays remain; any midnight lag missingness receives the fixed train-only treatment specified there. All four windows or benchmark unavailable; no best-window selection. LEAR−N/M and arm−LEAR are secondary. Seven months departs from Lago's at-least-one-year recommendation (PDF p.10).

P_shift and P_content are **PROJECT_CHOICE_NO_TEMPLATE**, secondary diagnostics. They test the L_chen representation for news and L_chen_notice for notices; no search over representations. Shift is exactly +28 days with publication/edit/saved-version/notice-registration clocks **and every derived event date** shifted together. Shifted own activity M_shift is recomputed on the same shifted support; comparisons with unshifted M are explicitly timing diagnostics. No wrapping future records back to the past. Original quotations remain immutable; parsed date projections shift, unknown remains unknown, and unparsed temporal content is reported.

P_content follows REDESIGN §4: retain source slot clocks, count, missing/failure status, region and target calendar; select donor content from different bundles already visible before the current origin, stratified by region×AEST year-month×weekday×exact activity count, fixed RNG seed 20261001. At least two distinct earlier eligible donor bundles excluding self are required. Donor event dates transfer by their publication-relative offset. No fallback to a broader stratum or to the unchanged self bundle. Unmatched slots are reported, never treated as a valid null. Paired diagnostics use only identical matched keys, with M's predictions on those keys; no valid coverage yields NO_VALID_PERMUTATION. A causal past-only reassignment cannot be a global one-to-one permutation including the first bundle; donor reuse across origins is disclosed. It is an activity-matched causal reassignment adaptation of the project permutation rule, not a formal randomisation test or mechanism identification.

E3 on the notice window is secondary: NP=N+the frozen corrected E3 P5MIN block; NPL=NP+M_notice+L14; NPP=NP+its corresponding shifted activity+the all-clock/event-date-shifted L14. P5MIN enters **only** these E3 arms, under the original run/target/availability contract, never main N. E3 asks whether the former positive finding recurs under new extraction; it cannot by itself isolate content beyond volume. Report NPL−NP, NPL−NPP, NP−N and NPP−NP; the secondary joint reading requires favorable reliable NPL−NP and NPL−NPP, otherwise the earlier positive result is not confirmed by this design. No extra margin or equivalence gate is imported from C.

## 6. Outcomes, families and prewritten reading

See INFERENCE_RULES.md and code/scoring.py for exact algebra. For each task separately, form per-day means of **paired p=1 absolute loss differences** on common eligible keys with unit evaluation weights. Primary news has 214 daily units; notice has 61 target-day units. Main keys are shared across all main arms; distinguish per-origin/region coverage loss and any arm-specific unavailable configuration. Primary inference requires the entire registered common finite/legal label grid: 72 region-hour keys per news day (15,408 total) and 8,640 region-horizon keys per notice target day (527,040 total). Thus daily averages and pooled unit-row MAE agree. Missing target truth, an illegal/unresolved anchor, an unmatched prediction or a missing whole day prevents a complete-window primary reading: report the gate failure plus available descriptive results, without shortening the population or dropping the hypothesis from Holm. Missing numeric predictors use the frozen native-NaN/mask treatment and by themselves do not discard a target key.

Primary DM is Lago's daily statistic sqrt(n)×mean(d)/sampleSD(d), standard-normal calibration, reported with both directional tails and **two-sided p** for the family decision. Holm at .05 applies separately to {L_count−M,L_chen−M} and {L14−M_notice,L_Laz−M_notice,L_chen_notice−M_notice}. Report original family sizes even if an arm is unavailable; unavailable p is conservatively 1 for family correction and the hypothesis is marked NOT_EVALUABLE. The selected sign assigns improvement/deterioration after two-sided rejection, avoiding two unallocated directional families. Conditional covariance-stationarity/asymptotic assumptions are stated. HAC7/Bartlett DM is a sensitivity, not a replacement chosen by significance.

Every contrast reports 100×(MAE_A−MAE_reference)/MAE_reference and a paired 95% interval. Primary interval is 2,000 one-day bootstrap resamples; seven-day non-circular moving blocks are the sole block sensitivity. Each draw recomputes the reference denominator from the same resampled daily losses; zero/nonfinite denominator is undefined. Reset default_rng(20260925) per block length and use common picks across eligible contrasts. Save draws, picks, finite counts and nominal linear-quantile intervals. Percent intervals are conditional on these fixed predictions and sample; they are not confidence intervals across all possible training procedures or simultaneous Holm-adjusted bounds.

Exact primary language, substituted with the arm/reference and numeric values:

* Holm rejection with favorable sign: “A shows a conditional reliable increment beyond [article/notice] activity under the registered daily DM rule: Δ = D% of reference MAE, 95% interval [L%, U%].” If the bootstrap interval crosses zero or HAC7/7-day results disagree, append: “The pre-specified uncertainty methods disagree; this finding is not robust to all registered dependence checks.”
* Holm rejection with unfavorable sign: “A shows conditional reliable deterioration relative to reference under the registered daily DM rule: Δ = D%, 95% interval [L%, U%].” Apply the same disagreement qualifier.
* No rejection: “No reliable increment beyond [article volume/notice activity] was detected under the registered family rule; Δ = D% of reference MAE, 95% interval [L%, U%].” If L<0, append “Improvements larger than X% of reference MAE are excluded by this nominal 95% interval,” where X=−L. If L≥0, append “The nominal 95% interval permits no positive improvement; it does not establish equivalence.” State that exclusion is a fixed-prediction, method-specific bound, not a global or margin-based claim. With undefined intervals, no exclusion bound is asserted.
* Missing input/source/variance/complete-day gate: “This registered contrast is not evaluable because [fixed gate]; no confirmation conclusion is drawn.” Keep the family entry and all failed evidence.

There is **no equivalence margin** for these windows, no TOST and no equivalence claim. Non-significance is not zero or no information value. The percentage reference is M for activity contrasts, E_raw for L_chen−E_raw, N for arm−N, NP/NPP for their E3 contrasts, and each stated placebo/benchmark reference. Do not substitute N's MAE for M-based effects.

All remaining contrasts are secondary: E_raw−M, W_Bai−M, L_chen−E_raw, L_chen−W_Bai, every available arm−N, R/T and representation comparisons, placebos, LEAR, E3, 24h, first-L and first-seven-day exclusion. Report Δ/interval and unadjusted daily DM p with “secondary/descriptive; does not change the primary family decision.” Secondary significant results may be described as conditional secondary evidence, never as a rescued primary confirmation. The secondary E3 joint gate, placebo agreement/disagreement and seed-instability wording are fixed in INFERENCE_RULES.md. No horizon/region/event subgroup is promoted to primary.

## 8. Budget, priorities and execution handoff

COMPUTE_PLAN.md fixes fit counts, runtime evidence/scenarios and drop order. News uses v9's fixed four single-thread workers with a barrier after every origin, mmap/chunked bank access and fresh fit workers. Preserve a four-worker ceiling, with memory admission/stop checks; a previous pilot exceeded 2 GiB, so four workers cannot be assumed safe without reduced matrix copies and measured RSS. Notice fits run serially with their inherited six threads. LEAR's many small hourly/window fits are counted separately from pooled LightGBM fits. Encoding, data preparation and extraction are not fitted models and their time is reported separately; no claim of measured 2026 hours is made.

The fitting cap is approximately 24 wall-clock hours summed over active fitting stages (including validation, pilots and unfinished attempts). First complete the two primary tasks; then fixed secondary runs; then extra seeds and sensitivities. Before starting a complete configuration, use the fixed reserve/timing rule in COMPUTE_PLAN.md. A budget failure never changes dates, origin order, primary families or accuracy rules; drop remaining lower-priority entire configurations in the frozen order, label them NOT_RUN_BUDGET, retain any partial predictions without scoring. A primary shortfall is CONFIRMATION_INCOMPLETE_BUDGET, not a null result. No outcome-dependent early stopping.

The later execution task must resolve only documented engineering/input gates: final NRX per-notice extraction receipt and new-schema success/failure partition, canonical NOTICE clock/coverage manifest, normalized NEWSX batch/transport receipt, Jan–Feb2026 news training bridge, required E3 source/input schema, encoder weights, and immutable source hashes.
